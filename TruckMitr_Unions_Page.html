The Hard Truth

India's Truck Drivers Are Being
Left Behind. Silently.

Every year, India's unions fight hard for drivers. But without a unified platform, the fight remains fragmented — state by state, city by city — while the problems keep growing.

⚠️ 30 Lakh+

Driver Shortage Right Now

India faces a crisis-level deficit of trained truck drivers today — and this number is growing every year as demand for freight rises and driver attrition accelerates.

💸 78%

Drivers Have No Formal Insurance

Despite being the backbone of India's supply chain, nearly 4 in 5 truck drivers have no adequate health or life insurance — leaving families exposed to catastrophic risk.

🏦 65%

Trapped in Debt Cycles

The majority of drivers remain financially vulnerable — exploited by unregulated lenders, paid unfairly by brokers, and unable to access formal banking or credit.

⏰ 14–18 hrs

Average Daily Driving Hours

Drivers routinely operate vehicles for 14–18 hours per day — far beyond safe limits — driven by trip-based payment models that punish rest and reward recklessness.

🏥 3x

Higher Health Risk Than Average Worker

Truck drivers face three times the risk of cardiovascular disease, diabetes, musculoskeletal disorders, and respiratory illness compared to the average Indian worker.

🏛️ Zero

No Formal Government Driver Board

Despite decades of demands, India still has no government-recognised Driver Welfare Board with statutory power — no formal seat at the policy table for the trucking workforce.

Drivers pushed beyond human limits by trip incentives, route pressure, and fear of job loss are operating multi-tonne vehicles at the edge of consciousness. This is not a driving problem — it is a systemic exploitation problem that no regulation has fixed because drivers have no unified voice to demand it change.

👁️ Severely Under-Reported 👁️

Eye Health — A Crisis Nobody Talks About

Glare from oncoming traffic, dust on highways, UV exposure through windshields, and night driving for 10+ hours a day are devastating driver eyesight at an alarming rate. Studies suggest over 60% of truck drivers above age 40 have uncorrected vision impairment — yet have no access to regular eye screenings, corrective lenses, or specialist care. A driver going blind slowly is a risk to every life on the road.

🧠 The Crisis No One Speaks About 🧠

Mental Health — India's Most Ignored Driver Crisis

Picture a man who leaves his family on a Monday morning and does not return home for 3 weeks. He eats alone at a dhaba. He sleeps in his cabin. He is shouted at by transporters, disrespected at toll nakas, and cheated by brokers. He earns money but cannot save it. He worries about his children's education, his wife's health, and his own body — which aches every single day.

This is not the story of one driver. This is the story of 10 million drivers. And not one mainstream policy document in India has called this what it is: a mental health emergency hiding in plain sight on the nation's highways.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Separation

Months away from home. Children growing up without their father present. Marriages strained beyond breaking point. Loneliness so deep it feels like exile.

😤 Chronic Humiliation

Disrespected at factories, ignored at toll plazas, cheated by clearing agents. A workforce with no dignity, no grievance mechanism, and no platform to speak from.

💰 Financial Anxiety

Irregular income, no savings, high-interest loans, no PF, no safety net. The psychological burden of financial precarity 365 days a year with zero support.

😔 Depression & Isolation

Surveys show over 35% of long-haul drivers display clinical symptoms of depression — but stigma, lack of access, and zero awareness mean almost none seek help.

😰 Anxiety & PTSD

Witnessing accidents, driving through dangerous conditions, facing criminal threats on highways — traumatic experiences that pile up with zero psychological support.

🚫 Zero Support Systems

No counselling services. No helplines. No mental health awareness. No employer responsibility. No government program. A crisis met with complete silence.

🏥 Physical Health Burden 🫀

The Body Pays the Price
